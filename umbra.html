Umbra

Umbra is a Kaiku club residency curated by Carlina Carpelan in 2026, focusing on hypnotic, leftfield and groove-driven electronic music.

The residency brings together carefully selected local and international artists, creating immersive nights centered around deep listening, physical rhythm and underground club culture.
