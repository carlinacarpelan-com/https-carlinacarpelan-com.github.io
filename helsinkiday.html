Urban Folks United

Together with our activist collective and Dodo ry, we organised a discussion panel at Kääntöpöytä on Helsinki Day, 13 June 2026.

The discussion explored low-threshold community building in urban environments and the ways in which cities can support accessible, inclusive and resident-led communities.

Facilitator:
Mark Lepre

Panellists:
Mia Haglund, Helsinki City Councillor, Left Alliance
Eero Laaksonen, Sompasauna

Location:
Kääntöpöytä, Helsinki
