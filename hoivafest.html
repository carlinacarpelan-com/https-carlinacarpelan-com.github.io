Hoiva Fest

Together with our new activist collective, we are organising Hoiva Fest, a two-day festival dedicated to planetary care at the historic Lapinlahti Hospital in Helsinki.

The programme brings together talks, workshops, practices for mental and physical wellbeing, and multidisciplinary art performances, creating space for collective care, learning and community.

Location:
Lapinlahti Hospital, Helsinki

Format:
Two-day festival

Theme:
Planetary Care
