Hoiva Fest

Hoiva Fest is an open and free two-day celebration of care, art and community at the historic Lapinlahti hospital area in Helsinki.

The festival explores care and its place in society through science, art and embodied experience. The programme includes discussions, workshops, music, films and different forms of bodily care.

Hoiva Fest creates space for learning, encounters, creativity, relaxation and togetherness — inviting everyone to share ideas and experience the warmth of community, hope and care.
